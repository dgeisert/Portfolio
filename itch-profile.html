Hi, I'm David. I've been making games for 14 years, and my favourite part hasn't changed: watching someone play a thing I made and seeing the exact moment it clicks.

I started as a product manager at Zynga, designed systems for a run of mobile hits at Rocket Games, co-founded the studio behind Ninja Legends (one of the first Quest titles, $1M+ revenue), then spent five years prototyping AI and AR at Meta Reality Labs. These days I make games with AI, quickly and in the open.

Why I make games

A new technology isn't real until someone plays with it. AI can build a game in an afternoon, but it can't make it fun. That part still takes a person: trying the idea, putting it in front of players, watching where they get stuck, and cutting until what's left feels good. That loop is what I love, and it's why most of what's on this page started as a jam game.

Jamference

I founded and host Jamference, a free AI game jam that runs every two weeks. Each round has a theme and a mandatory design restriction: Movement Input Only, Playable on Mobile, No Text, Hidden Lucky Cat. The restriction is the point. It pushes people past the obvious use of AI and towards ideas nobody has played yet.

Everyone rates each other's games on Fun, AI Use, Polish and Theme, entrants share which AI tools they used and what each one did, and I record a playthrough of every entry with my feedback.

  • 112 games and 876 player ratings in four rounds, about 9 ratings per game
  • A small, very active Discord where builders keep coming back round after round
  • I enter every round too: Kitty Kingdom was rated Most Fun in #1, and Starry Meows won #3
